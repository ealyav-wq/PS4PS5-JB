Instructions

3) Cache Trainer (Go to Trainer Offline Link).
4) After Cache is 100% Successful, Close Browser and Re-Open it.
5) Launch your game on the PS4.
6) Click your game (with the matching CUSA/version) in the trainer.
7) If the trainer message says "Trainer Attached.", you can start cheating.
